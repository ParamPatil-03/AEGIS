WHY INDIA?

  • India sits within the Equatorial Ionization Anomaly — one of the most volatile ionospheric regions on Earth
  • GAGAN and NavIC — India's own satellite navigation systems — are directly exposed to EIA-driven TEC disruption
  • Existing global space weather models are not trained on Indian ground station data and miss EIA-specific behavior
